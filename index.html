03 MSS Quality —
Q1 — On the MSS candle close beyond the swing high or low: does Pepperstone show a confirmed body close while IC Markets shows a wick sweep, or vice versa — the two platforms disagree?
Q2 — Did the M15 impulse leg tap into a D1 Fair Value Gap within the Daily impulse leg or Daily pullback leg, and reject from it?
Q3 — Did the rejecting leg sweep the DBI high or low, or a high or low from 2 to 3 days prior, in the process of that rejection?
Q4 — Did the M15 impulse leg tap into a Weekly (W1) Fair Value Gap and reject from it?
Q5 — Did the rejecting leg also sweep a structural level — a weekly high or low or a significant prior swing — in the process of that W1 FVG rejection?
Reference
Broker inconsistency (Q1)
Pepperstone and IC Markets disagree on whether the MSS candle closed as a body beyond the swing high or low, or swept it as a wick. One confirms the body close, the other reads it as a wick. Both platforms are already open during analysis — this check is instantaneous.
D1 FVG (Q2)
A fair value gap on the Daily timeframe within the Daily impulse leg or Daily pullback leg. Not the same as a D1 Decisional or Extreme zone. The M15 impulse leg taps this gap and the market turns — confirming non-acceptance from a higher timeframe inefficiency.
DBI or 2–3 day sweep (Q3)
The rejecting leg clears the Day-Before-It's high or low, or a high or low from 2 to 3 days prior. Only checked when Q2 is confirmed. Determines whether the condition escalates from medium flag to hard kill.
W1 FVG (Q4)
A fair value gap on the Weekly timeframe. The M15 impulse leg taps this gap and rejects from it. Carries medium flag weight. Higher timeframe than D1 FVG so the opposing energy is stronger.
W1 structural sweep (Q5)
The rejecting leg sweeps a weekly high or low or significant prior swing. Only checked when Q4 is confirmed. Escalates W1 FVG rejection from medium flag to hard kill — compound signature.
Outcome map
Q1 No, Q2 No, Q4 No → clean pass. Q1 Yes, Q2 No → medium flag. Q2 Yes, Q3 No → medium flag. Q2 Yes, Q3 Yes → hard kill. Q4 Yes, Q5 No → medium flag. Q4 Yes, Q5 Yes → hard kill.
